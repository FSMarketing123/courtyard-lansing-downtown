Investment Overview

Hodges Ward Elliott (“HWE”) is pleased to present the opportunity to acquire the fee-simple condominium interest in the 122-room Courtyard Lansing Downtown (“Property” or “Hotel”). The Marriott-branded, premium select-service hotel, was recently constructed in 2021 and is located at 600 E. Michigan Avenue at the heart of downtown Lansing.

The Hotel is surrounded by Lansing's Stadium District, home to a variety of prominent government, university, healthcare, sports, and leisure demand generators. The Hotel is directly adjacent to Capital City Market and across from Jackson Field, the Hotel offers convenient walkable access to Lansing Center (0.3 miles), Michigan State University (2.4 miles), the Michigan State Capitol (0.6 miles), and University of Michigan Health–Sparrow Hospital (0.4 miles).

Investment Highlights

Investment Highlight 01

New-Build Product at a Compelling Rooms-Revenue Multiple

Built in 2021, the Property offers investors a modern Courtyard design that distinguishes it from much of Lansing's older lodging inventory. A new owner can expect minimal PIP requirements and avoid near-term disruption to operations, allowing for a focus solely on high ROI improvements to guest-facing areas. The Property also offers investors a compelling all-in basis relative to its rooms-revenue generation, and recent / current construction cost.

Investment Highlight 02

Clean Investment Opportunity

The fee-simple condominium interest in the Courtyard is being offered free and clear of existing management and financing, affording new ownership maximum optionality in the future operation and capitalization of the Hotel.

Investment Highlight 03

Best-in-Class Brand Affiliation

Marriott International is arguably the strongest brand in the lodging industry, and Courtyard is one of its most widely recognized brands, often achieving outsized market share and investment returns for franchisees. At the time of publication, the Courtyard system includes more than 1,000 properties and 140,000 guestrooms across the United States and Canada.

Marriott provides a highly rated reservation system and access to more than 270 million active Marriott Bonvoy members. Marriott is known to have a tremendously loyal customer following and appeals to a variety of consumer segments that provide strong room-night demand every day of the week.

Investment Highlight 05

Proximity to Michigan State University and Record-Level Enrollment

The Property benefits from its excellent central core location within the greater Lansing–East Lansing market and proximity to Michigan State University, one of the nation's largest public research universities and a major year-round lodging-demand generator. MSU reported a total of 51,838 students in Fall 2025, including an all-time record of 10,720 new undergraduates.

Investment Highlight 06

Downtown Location Near the Michigan State Capitol

Located in the heart of Michigan's capital city, the Property benefits from lodging demand generated by the Michigan Legislature, executive agencies, courts, associations, lobbyists, contractors, and other organizations that regularly conduct business with state government. Its downtown location places guests near the Michigan State Capitol and the concentration of government offices surrounding the Capitol Complex.
